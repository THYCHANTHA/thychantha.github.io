About me

I turn data into forecasts, dashboards and machine learning that people can actually use.

I am a Data Science graduate of the Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC), with an engineer's degree in Applied Mathematics and Statistics (GPA 3.5). My thesis, graded A, used Power Platform, Power BI and machine learning to digitalise a company's finance workflow. I now work as a Junior Data Analyst at Comin Khmere Co., Ltd. on Power BI, Power Platform and SAP, and I teach Power BI and Python as a trainer.

I am most interested in data analysis and machine learning for public statistics: agricultural forecasting, data integration and careful model evaluation. My next step is a master's degree in data analysis and machine learning, to go deeper into the methods behind this work.
