Caffeine Consumptions by University Students

A comprehensive study on caffeine intake among 200 university students, analyzing consumption habits, academic performance, and health impacts.

Open full page View PDF

Problem

Students may not clearly understand how caffeine habits relate to study patterns, sleep, and health. The goal was to use data analysis to reveal patterns and support healthier recommendations.

Overview

This research study analyzes caffeine intake patterns among 200 university students, covering consumption habits, academic performance, and health effects using exploratory data analysis and statistical methods.

Methodology

  • Survey-based data collection (200 participants)
  • Exploratory data analysis (EDA)
  • Gender-based consumption comparison
  • Study-hour and performance correlation
  • Health-impact interpretation and recommendations

Technologies

  • Data Analysis
  • Statistics
  • Health Research
  • Python

Results

Identified clear consumption patterns among students and produced evidence-based recommendations for healthier caffeine intake.
