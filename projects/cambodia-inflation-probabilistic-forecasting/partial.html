Probabilistic Forecasting of Phnom Penh Inflation

A frozen research protocol and tested software for probabilistic forecasts of Phnom Penh consumer price inflation at 1, 3, 6 and 12 month horizons.

Open full page View on GitHub

Problem

Do probabilistic forecasting methods give better-calibrated and sharper predictive distributions than conventional benchmarks, and does forecast uncertainty change across inflation regimes?

Overview

A time-series study of forecast uncertainty. The target is year-on-year inflation of the NIS Consumer Price Index for Phnom Penh, never described as national inflation.

Methodology

  • Data source audit and a rights review before any use
  • Benchmarks: persistence, rolling mean and a known-component plus seasonal-mean benchmark
  • Expanding-window rolling-origin evaluation with WIS (primary), CRPS, pinball loss, coverage and sharpness
  • Diebold-Mariano (HLN) tests and block bootstrap

Technologies

  • Python
  • Time Series
  • Probabilistic Forecasting
  • Forecast Evaluation

Results

In progress. The protocol (v1.0) is frozen, the software is tested on synthetic data and exploratory figures are produced. No model has been fitted to real data and there are no forecasting results yet.
