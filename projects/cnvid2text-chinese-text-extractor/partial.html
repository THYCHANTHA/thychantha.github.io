CNVid2Text: Chinese Text Extractor

A Streamlit app that extracts Chinese text from video pages via URL or QR code, with text statistics, history and TXT/JSON export.

Open full page View on GitHub

Problem

Copying Chinese text out of video pages by hand is slow.

Overview

Paste a video-page URL or upload a QR code image containing one, and the app decodes it, extracts the Chinese text and shows statistics.

Methodology

  • QR decoding to a video URL
  • Text extraction and analysis
  • Extraction history, TXT and JSON downloads
  • Streamlit interface with an alternative Flask version

Technologies

  • Python
  • Streamlit
  • Flask
  • QR Code
