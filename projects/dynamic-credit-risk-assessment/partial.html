Dynamic Credit Risk Assessment System

An interactive multilingual credit risk assessment platform using Streamlit and Transformers to analyze loan applications in Khmer and English with real-time risk simulation.

Open full page View on GitHub

Problem

Loan review often requires manual interpretation of applicant information and risk indicators across languages. The goal was to support more dynamic, transparent, and multilingual risk analysis.

Overview

This platform helps analyze loan applications in Khmer and English using Streamlit and Transformer-based NLP, with real-time risk simulation to support more transparent, multilingual credit decisions.

Methodology

  • Multilingual risk assessment (Khmer and English)
  • Zero-shot classification with XLM-RoBERTa
  • Interactive Streamlit interface
  • Real-time risk simulation
  • Plotly-based risk visualization

Technologies

  • Streamlit
  • XLM-RoBERTa
  • Plotly
  • NLP
  • Zero-Shot Classification

Results

Demonstrates how multilingual NLP and interactive analytics can support clearer, faster credit risk review.
