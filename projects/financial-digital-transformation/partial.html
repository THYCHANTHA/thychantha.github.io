Final Thesis: Financial Digital Transformation via Power Platform and Machine Learning

My engineering thesis at ITC (defended July 2026): digitalising a company finance workflow with Power Apps, SharePoint and Power Automate, reporting it in Power BI, and testing a leakage-safe machine learning model that flags high-amount expense claims for review.

Open full page Read the full thesis report View defence slides

Problem

Manual or semi-digital finance processes are slow to track, hard to audit and leave data unstructured. The thesis asks how a finance workflow can become structured digital data that supports approval tracking, management reporting and data science, and whether machine learning can responsibly support the finance team without replacing human approval.

Overview

Final-year thesis for the Bachelor of Engineering in Data Science at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia (Department of Applied Mathematics and Statistics), advised by Mr. KHEAN Vesal. The work was carried out during my internship at Comin Khmere Co., Ltd. (12 February to 11 June 2026). The company's finance digitalisation covers 11 finance forms; the thesis studies the Expense Claim form in depth as the main case study and presents the other forms briefly.

Methodology

  • Process analysis of the Expense Claim workflow, then a digital form built in Power Apps with data stored in SharePoint lists
  • Approval routing, email notifications and status updates automated with Power Automate
  • Power Query transformation, data modelling and DAX measures feeding an interactive Power BI dashboard (claims, total and average amount, approval status, department, project, expense type, high-amount claims)
  • A synthetic Expense Claim dataset of 5,300 records built from the real form and SharePoint schema, so no company data is exposed
  • Leakage control: amount-related, approval-result and workflow-status fields removed from the model inputs; stratified 80/20 split (4,240 train, 1,060 test), 5-fold cross-validation and threshold tuning from 0.10 to 0.90
  • Five classifiers compared (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Balanced Random Forest, Extra Trees, Gradient Boosting) and selected with a recall-weighted business score

Key features

  • Digital finance forms with multi-level approvals and automated notifications
  • Power BI dashboards for finance monitoring
  • Decision-support model that flags claims needing closer review (not an automatic approver)
  • Recommendations on data standards, KPI validation and phased roll-out

Results

  • Final model: Gradient Boosting on the cleaned, leakage-safe feature set, threshold 0.46
  • Accuracy 91.89%, precision 86.23%, recall 89.68%, F1-score 87.92%, ROC-AUC 97.71% and an 88.91% business score on the held-out test data
  • Target: High Amount Claim, defined as a grand total of USD 300 or more

The finance workflow becomes structured, trackable data with clearer approval visibility and dashboard reporting. The model results come from a synthetic dataset, so they show the method's potential rather than performance on live company data, and the model is designed to assist reviewers, never to approve or reject claims.
