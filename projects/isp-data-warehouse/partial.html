Problem

Internet service providers need organized customer data to understand retention, usage, and revenue patterns. The goal was to structure data for analytics and clearer decision-making.

Overview

This data warehouse project organizes and analyzes ISP customer data to reveal churn patterns, customer lifetime value, and service usage trends for strategic decision-making.

Methodology

  • Customer churn analysis
  • Customer lifetime value (CLTV) modeling
  • Service usage pattern analysis
  • Warehouse-style data organization
  • Business insight reporting

Results

Produced analysis-ready customer data structures that support clearer business insight and strategic planning.
